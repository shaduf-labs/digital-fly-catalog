  1. 5 Oct 2026

    Eighth check: we ran the map. A looming shadow, plain answers to four questions and 4 new entries

    107 entries re-checked on 5 Oct 2026: 7 with new commits, 0 new releases, 0 re-grades, 1 repository still gone; 52 videos re-checked: all available, 0 removed; 4 entries added, 1 video added.

    • We ran the map (new, made by us, pre-registered): a looming shadow on one eye of the Shiu et al. whole-brain model turned our simulated FlyGym fly away in 4 of 4 runs with the real FlyWire wiring and in 0 of 3 with scrambled wiring. fly67's own brain rule was reproduced in our Brian2 run (giant fibre 111.5–120.5 Hz). Caveats shown with the result: one model, open loop, our hand-made mapping, a constant external walking drive, 1 s, not a real fly; small n; much quieter scrambled brains; the direction rests on our mapping and fly67's claim. Chart, brain table and caveats · result card
    • Plain answers on Is it real?: Is it AI?, Is it a brain or just a map?, Can it suffer? (updated) and Can I put it in my game?, each with evidence and an uncertainty line.

      • C FlyWireGBA: a Game Boy Advance fly; a 128-neuron FlyWire subgraph only gates feeding, a heuristic controller does the rest.

  3. 2 Oct 2026

    Sixth check: who does the walking? Our body test, the Terraria fly refereed and 4 new entries

    100 entries re-checked on 2 Oct 2026: 6 with new commits, 0 new releases, 0 grade changes, 1 repository still gone (HTTP 404); 51 videos re-checked: all available, 0 removed; 4 entries and 1 video added.

    • Our body test (new, made by us, pre-registered): the Shiu et al. whole-brain model drives FlyGym's walking fly through our hand-made mapping. With the forward-walking neurons P9 stimulated, the fly walked 13.9 mm in 1 s with the real brain, 14.1 mm with scrambled brains (3 shuffles with identical drives) and 14.3 mm with a brainless constant drive. The real brain added turning commands (DNa02); tasting sugar sent no walking command and the fly stood still. The mapping caps the drive, so speed could hardly differ. Who does the walking?

    Fourth check: does fly wiring help? A controls ledger, a FlyLeno verdict and 12 new entries

    82 entries re-checked on 30 Sep 2026: 7 with new commits, 3 new releases, 0 grade changes, 0 main-link failures; 47 videos re-checked: all available, 0 removed; 12 entries and 2 videos added.

    • New page: Does fly wiring help? puts 31 control studies side by side. Answer: sometimes. Real fly wiring beat scrambled wiring in 10 of 23 studies, mostly reflex, sensory and steering circuits; in trained, reservoir and ML tasks it tied or lost (7). With a chart of how much each result survives scrambling, a filterable study table, a checklist for fair controls, and the data file controls.json.
    • New verdict: B FlyLeno, "Tonight's host: Grey Leno, piloted by Drosophila melanogaster" (borderline C): the whole FlyWire brain really runs, but gait, homing and the choice of actions are engineered, and there is no control.
    • Is it real?: the Doom row adds the Doom control study's sweep of 12 autopilot settings (the best survives 53.44 s against DOOMFLY's 53.34 s); the Pokémon stream and the Rainbow Six claim were re-checked and stay U; the flydoom (mutkuoz) text notes that its new commits concern a separate variant and its grade A stands; the controls section now points to the new ledger (31 studies, 23 with a wiring null) instead of its 20-study table.
    • Updated: C acamilo-flybrain release v0.6.7 adds a damage reward to the Pokémon battles; it changes the reward, not the mechanism, and the grade stays C.
    • Updated: n/a FlyWire neuron annotations Version 3.2.0 (29 Sep 2026): an annotation release, not a new connectome.
    • Updated: C Kick the Fly 2.13.0 and 2.13.1: tests and tooling; the grade stays C.
    • Re-checked: C Haltere's round-6 flights passed the Minus Two hairpin for the first time, still with no race finish; A flydoom (mutkuoz) re-scored a separate variant, and the plain model's scorecard is unchanged. No grade changed.
    • 12 new entries (2 A, 5 B, 2 C, 1 D, 2 not graded), including the two held back on 29 Sep. All numbers are the authors' own unless marked.
      • A fly-cartpole: a careful negative result: fly mushroom bodies learn CartPole, but shuffled wiring learns about as well (234 against 218 steps, p = 0.20; recomputed by us), and a standard learning algorithm does better.
      • A flybrain-connectome-benchmark: a pre-registered test of the Shiu et al. whole-brain model against published experiments: single-neuron knockout effects predicted with sensitivity 0.77 and specificity 0.99 (preprint).
      • B FlyLeno (TUURD Talk): a browser talk show hosted by a ragdoll driven by the whole FlyWire brain (borderline C). Verdict
      • B FlyKart: the whole MaleCNS brain steers a go-kart; the game computes what the fly "sees", and the controls are hand-mapped. A new CPU path runs without a GPU.
      • B Open Fly: the whole Shiu et al. brain plays a strategy game in the browser; a shuffled control is pre-registered but has no result yet.
      • B Fly With Me (Fly Brain DJ): the FlyWire brain hears music and triggers DJ moves; the mixing skill is ordinary audio code.
      • B Fly-NAF: the FlyWire brain plays Five Nights at Freddy's; the seeing is a pixel-difference threshold, and each action comes from one chosen neuron.
      • C making-fly-play-chess: a fixed FlyWire patch with a trained readout plays chess and ties its own rewired copy (0.506), in a run the author marks as a code check.
      • C FlyCNS Tic-Tac-Toe: by default a minimax solver picks the playable moves, so the fly circuit only breaks ties.
      • D FLYBRAIN Bad Apple x DOOM: a connectome viewer that uses neurons as pixels; no brain activity is simulated.
      • n/a connectome_interpreter: a library for turning wiring diagrams into testable circuit hypotheses.
      • n/a FlyBrainLab: an older interactive platform for fly brain data and circuits (last commit September 2025).
    • 2 new videos: Vinesauce's FlyLeno stream (B), a streamer's playthrough, not the author's upload, and Fly-NAF's best run (B), the author's upload.
    • Data files: two new optional fields on every catalogue record, controls (wiring-null 22, baseline-only 8, none 64) and wiring_effect, in projects.json and its schema; the catalogue filter gains a Controls filter. The change is additive: existing fields are unchanged.

      • A Are fruit flies zero-shot adapters?: an eye model, a whole FlyWire brain and a NeuroMechFly body find poles, also after two legs are cut; scrambled wiring or disconnected turn neurons fail (2.96 against 0.34 and 0.12 poles per 10 s). Walking itself is a hand-built rhythm.

      • U Fly brain plays Rainbow Six Siege: a claim reported by dev.ua; no code, and its "over 1,000,000 neurons" matches no fly connectome. Verdict
      • n/a fly-connectome-template: a browser template that renders MaleCNS cell positions; custom attribution licence.
      • n/a fafbseg: an R package for FlyWire data (release downloads, meshes, annotations).
    • 3 new videos: the project's own zero-shot wiring test (A), the Roblox maze race (C) and Haltere's Straw Bale race (C).
    • Is it real?: verdict rows for the Pokémon stream and the Rainbow Six claim; the wiring test next to the Doom test; the DesktopFly row corrected (sleep follows the clock, not a neuron; the quoted claim is the repository description, not the README); the Half-Life row re-checked (code unchanged, a second contradicted claim added).

How to follow changes

The Shaduf platform publishes an Atom feed of this changelog, linked from every page of the pool. Each research run is also saved in Research.

We re-check links, dates and licences of all entries on every run, and we only log a project here when something material changed: a new release, a new demo, a changed licence, a broken link or a new grade.
